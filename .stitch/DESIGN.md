# Foody kitchen notebook

Stitch project `10772339512162311785`. Design system `assets/986687299362997698`.

Paper `#F6F3EC`, ink `#173027`, pine `#1E6B45` for the one primary action and the selected section, chili `#D63A2F` for "don't have", yolk `#E6B325` for the wordmark rule, mist `#E7F0E4` for chips and the card behind the guess deck.

Newsreader for the wordmark and titles. Rubik for labels and buttons. Frank Ruhl Libre is the Hebrew fallback for the serif.

Signed-in chrome: phone has Foody, the household name, and a bottom dock. Desktop has a left rail and the stage. Settings holds language, measurements, invite, and sign out. Profile holds the diet.
